LARFFAN BELEFU - 15 JOKES
Say: "Read number 1" or "Read next"

Try: "Read danfo", "Read next joke", "Stop"

1. DANFO CONDUCTOR

In Lagos, you don't need gym. The conductor will shout, "Enter with your change!" He already knows you don't have change. It's a trap.

2. NEIGHBOR GENERATOR

In Nigeria, we don't need alarm. My neighbor's generator will wake you by 5am. It says, "I HAVE LIGHT, YOU DON'T!" My radio is live, but his generator is louder.

3. CHARGE PHONE

We have 5 power banks, 2 generators, and 1 small solar. And still, phone is at 2 percent. We are professional phone chargers.

4. NIGERIAN MUM CALL

If your mum calls you once and you don't pick, she will call 10 times. The 11th call is from your dad. You are in trouble.

5. POWER BANK TRAVELED

My friend borrowed my power bank last year. He is now in Canada. The power bank is also in Canada. I am still in Lagos.

6. BUYING LAPTOP

I told the seller, "How much last?" He said, "Last na 300k." I said, "Oga abeg." He said, "Oya 299k." We negotiated for 2 hours for 1000 naira.

7. I'M COMING LIE

If a Nigerian says, "I'm coming, 2 minutes," he is still in bed. If he says, "I'm close," he just left his house. It's our culture.

8. SMALL PIKIN TIKTOK

My 3-year-old son knows how to scroll TikTok. He even knows how to skip my video. He said, "Daddy, this one no funny."

9. WIFI LIKE RELATIONSHIP

Sometimes it connects. Sometimes it doesn't. Sometimes it says, "Connected, no internet." Just like my ex.

10. RICE AND GAS

I put rice on fire. Gas finished. I put it on electric cooker. Light went off. At the end, we ate bread. Rice is still in the pot.

11. OKADA MAN PRICE

I told Okada man, "How much to go to stadium road?" He said, "500." I said, "Oga, 200." He said, "Oya enter." After 2 minutes, he said, "Oga, fuel don cost, add 300." We fight for road.

12. CHURCH CONTRIBUTION

In church, when they say, "Bring your offering," everybody brings big money. When they say, "Bring for building project," everybody is looking at ceiling. Holy Ghost will build am.

13. MY BARBER AND NETWORK

My barber said, "I go barb you fine." Light went off. He brought phone torchlight. Network went off. He said, "Oga, na spiritual barbing be this. Close eye."

14. HOW WE GREET IN PORT HARCOURT

In Port Harcourt, we don't say, "How are you?" We say, "How far? You don chop?" If you say you never chop, they will say, "Oya, na your pocket dey hungry, no be you."

15. RADIO SHOW AND NEPA

I started my live radio show, "Larffan Belefu Live." I shouted, "Good evening, Port Harcourt!" NEPA took light. My listeners heard, "Good evening, Port Har... kpa!" Darkness.
