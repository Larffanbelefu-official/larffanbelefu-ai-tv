1. DANFO CONDUCTOR

In Lagos, you do not need to go to the gym. The bus conductor will shout, enter with your change. He already knows you do not have change. It is a trap.

2. NEIGHBOR GENERATOR

In Nigeria, we do not need an alarm clock. My neighbor's generator will wake you by five in the morning. It is saying, I have electricity and you do not.

3. CHARGING PHONE

We have five power banks, two generators, and one small solar panel. And still, the phone battery is at two percent. We are professional phone chargers.

4. NIGERIAN MOTHER CALL

If your mother calls you once and you do not answer, she will call ten times. The eleventh call will be from your father. You are in serious trouble.

5. POWER BANK TRAVELED

My friend borrowed my power bank last year. He is now in Canada. The power bank is also in Canada. I am still in Lagos.

6. BUYING LAPTOP

I asked the seller, what is the last price? He said, last price is three hundred thousand. I begged him. He said, okay, two hundred and ninety nine thousand. We negotiated for two hours for one thousand naira.

7. I AM COMING LIE

If a Nigerian says, I am coming in two minutes, he is still in bed. If he says, I am close, he just left his house. It is our culture.

8. SMALL CHILD TIKTOK

My three year old son knows how to scroll TikTok. He even knows how to skip my video. He said, daddy, this one is not funny.

9. WIFI LIKE RELATIONSHIP

Sometimes it connects. Sometimes it does not. Sometimes it says, connected, no internet. Just like my former relationship.

10. RICE AND GAS

I put rice on the fire. The gas finished. I put it on electric cooker. Electricity went off. At the end, we ate bread. The rice is still in the pot.

11. OKADA MAN PRICE

I asked the motorcycle rider, how much to Stadium Road? He said, five hundred naira. I said, two hundred. He said, enter. After two minutes, he said, fuel is expensive, add three hundred naira.

12. CHURCH CONTRIBUTION

In church, when they say, bring your offering, everybody brings big money. When they say, bring money for building project, everybody looks at the ceiling. They think the Holy Ghost will build it.

13. MY BARBER AND NETWORK

My barber said, I will give you a fine haircut. Electricity went off. He brought out phone torchlight. Network went off. He said, sir, this is a spiritual haircut. Please close your eyes.

14. HOW WE GREET IN PORT HARCOURT

In Port Harcourt, we do not say, how are you? We say, how far? Have you eaten? If you say you have not eaten, they will say, it is your pocket that is hungry, not you.

15. RADIO SHOW AND ELECTRICITY

I started my live radio show, Larffan Belefu Live. I shouted, good evening Port Harcourt. Electricity was cut off. My listeners heard, good evening Port Harcourt and then silence. Darkness everywhere.

16. BIG BIRD ON THE ROOF

Early in the morning, a big bird landed on my roof. My neighbor shouted, are you okay? I said, I am not okay. This bird wants to take my roof. The bird looked at me and said, sir, you have not paid your house rent, so I am now the landlord.

17. THE SMOKER QUESTION

A man was smoking at the junction. Another man asked him, are you okay? The smoker said, yes, I am okay. The man asked again, are you really okay? The smoker said, I said yes. If I am not okay, will you give me your lungs?

18. MONEY HIDING JOKE

Nigerian money plays hide and seek. When you need it, it hides. When you do not need it, it appears. My account balance always says, I will come later. That later has not come since January.

19. BANK TRANSFER JOKE

I sent money to my friend and I said, I have transferred. He said, I have not seen it. I checked my bank, they have removed the money. He checked his bank, they have not added the money. So where is the money? It is in the air having a honeymoon.

20. RICH MAN AND POOR MAN

A rich man sleeps with air conditioner, a poor man sleeps in heat. But when electricity is cut off, both of them sweat in darkness. Money cannot buy electricity in Nigeria. Only God can provide electricity.
